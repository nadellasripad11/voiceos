Voice assistant
at your fingertips

Speak anywhere you would otherwise type. VoiceOS turns what you say into finished text, and into actions across the apps you already use.

  • Runs on this machine. Speech recognition and every action happen locally. There is no server.
  • Costs nothing. No account, no subscription, no API bills.
  • Works everywhere. Any app with a normal text field.
